Your account

Delete your Wayraa account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, in any of these three ways. You don't need the app for the last two.

1

In the app

  1. Open Wayraa and sign in.
  2. Open Settings: the menu (☰) at the top right of Home, or Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom and confirm.

Deleted straight away.

2

On the website

  1. Sign in on wayraa.com.
  2. Open your account menu (your picture, top right) → Settings.
  3. Click Delete account and confirm.
Go to Settings

Deleted straight away.

3

By email

Write to {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }} from the email address on your account, asking us to delete it. If you can't, tell us that email address and we'll check it's yours first.

Email us

We'll confirm, and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, photo and sign-in.
  • Your stories and journals, and their photos.
  • Your favourites, and trips only you are on.
  • You're removed from other people's trips.

What happens to shared trips

Trips you own that other people are on aren't deleted: they're passed to another person on the trip, so their plans aren't lost. Things you added there (places, expenses, checklist items) stay part of that trip.

Backups

Copies in backups and server logs are removed over a short period afterwards. Deleting your account can't be undone.
