The story

Years of trying, finally real

I've always loved to travel, and since my college days I kept trying to build travel websites. There were several attempts before this one, and some of them failed. Wayraa is the one that finally came to life.

The reason was simple. Every time I planned a trip, it turned into Excel sheets, long discussions and plans that kept changing, and not everyone in the group knew what was decided. I faced those problems myself, and I always wanted to solve them.

So I built Wayraa, to help anyone plan a trip: find the places, put them into days, plan it together with friends, and keep the budget and checklist in one place.

Prabhat Padhiary
