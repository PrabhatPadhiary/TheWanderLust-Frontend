Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ LAST_UPDATED }}

Wayraa helps you plan trips with friends and share travel stories. This page explains what information we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: we use your information to run Wayraa, we don't sell it, and we don't show ads.

1. Who we are

"Wayraa", "we" and "us" mean the Wayraa mobile app and the website wayraa.com. For anything about your privacy, email {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}.

2. What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile photo. You sign in with Google or with an email and password. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your Google password.
  • Trips you plan: destinations and stops, dates, number of travellers, saved places, your day-by-day itinerary, notes, checklists, budgets and expenses (including who paid for what).
  • Stories and journals: what you write, the places you mention and the photos you add, and whether each one is public or private.
  • Photos you upload (profile, journals and stories).
  • Your location, only if you allow it and only while you're using the app, to show places near you and how far away they are. We use it for that search and don't keep a history of where you've been. You can turn it off any time in your phone's settings.
  • Other things you do in Wayraa: places you save as favourites, people you block, and content you report.
  • Technical information: like most online services, our servers record basic technical details such as your IP address, device and app version, and errors. We use these to keep Wayraa secure (for example, to stop abuse) and to fix problems.

3. How we use it

  • To run Wayraa: your account, your trips, your stories and the people you plan with.
  • To show place information, maps, photos and distances.
  • To keep your trip in sync for everyone on it, as changes happen.
  • To keep the community safe: handling reports, blocking, and removing content that breaks our rules.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't sell your information, we don't show ads, and we don't build advertising profiles.

4. Who can see what

  • Trips: the people on the trip. Anyone who has the trip's invite link can join, so share it only with people you trust; the trip owner can remove people and reset the link. People on a trip can see its plans, who made changes, and who has it open at the moment.
  • Public stories and journals: anyone using Wayraa. Private ones and drafts: only you.
  • Your name and photo are shown to people on your trips and next to the stories you publish.

5. Services we use

We rely on a few trusted companies to run Wayraa. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase for sign-in.
  • Google Maps Platform for place search, place details, photos and maps. Place searches you make are sent to Google. Google's use of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudinary to store and resize photos.
  • Render and Neon to host our servers and database.
  • Cloudflare to deliver our website.

These services may store information outside your country, for example in the United States or the European Union.

6. Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete account, or by emailing us. When you do:

  • your profile, your stories and journals, and their photos are deleted;
  • trips only you are on are deleted;
  • trips you own that others are on are passed to another person on the trip, so their plans aren't lost;
  • you're removed from other people's trips, and your sign-in is removed.

Copies in backups and server logs are removed over a short period afterwards.

7. Your choices

  • See and edit your profile in the app at any time.
  • Make a story private, or delete it.
  • Turn off location access in your phone's settings.
  • Block or report people and content.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law, and we'll respect them.

8. Children

Wayraa isn't meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age for online services where you live). If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we'll delete it.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), require sign-in for your trips, and limit access to your information to what's needed to run Wayraa. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will fix problems quickly.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and for important changes we'll let you know in the app.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: {{ SUPPORT_EMAIL }}.

4. Community rules

Don't post or share anything that:

  • is illegal, or encourages harm or violence;
  • harasses, threatens, bullies or targets anyone;
  • is hateful toward people for who they are;
  • is sexually explicit, or sexualises minors in any way;

You can report content and block people in the app. We may remove content and suspend or close accounts that break these rules.

5. Shared trips

Anyone with a trip's invite link can join that trip, so share it only with people you trust. The trip owner can remove people and reset the link. Editors can change the trip; viewers can only look at it.

6. Places and travel information

Place details, photos, opening hours, ratings and travel times come from Google and from other travellers, and may be wrong or out of date. Distances such as "2.5 km from centre" are measured in a straight line. Always check important details before you travel. Wayraa doesn't sell or book travel, and isn't responsible for your trips, bookings, or how costs are split in your group.

7. Google Maps

Wayraa uses Google Maps features and content. Your use of them is also subject to the Google Maps Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Using Wayraa fairly

Don't misuse Wayraa: for example, don't try to break into it, overload it, copy its data automatically (scraping), or use it to spam others.

9. Ending your use

You can stop using Wayraa and delete your account at any time. We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms.
